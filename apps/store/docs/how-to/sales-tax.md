# Sales tax

Reviewed November 24, 2026. General operating notes, not tax advice: confirm
filing questions with the accountant. The open setup steps are tracked in the
[roadmap](../planning/roadmap.md#4-shopify-and-printful-operating-setup).

## Who sells

The store belongs to **Ryan Meetup Incorporated**, a New York not-for-profit
corporation (Kings County, formed January 17, 2024). It has no IRS tax
exemption and is not a 501(c)(3), so federally it is taxed much like an
ordinary corporation. Shopify payouts, Printful costs and the sales-tax
registration all belong to the corporation, under its EIN.

Not-for-profit status does not exempt store sales: in New York, sales of goods
from a store run by an exempt organization are generally still taxable.

## Why the store collects but Etsy did not

Etsy is a **marketplace facilitator**. New York treats Etsy as the seller for
sales tax, so Etsy charges and remits tax on Etsy orders and the corporation
needs no registration for them. On this storefront the corporation is the
seller; Shopify only runs the software, so collection is the corporation's job.
Report only the store's own sales on New York returns.

## New York

1. **Register.** A free Certificate of Authority from the NY Department of
   Taxation and Finance (tax.ny.gov → Register for sales tax, through NY
   Business Express). Done: the corporation's certificate was issued after its
   November 21, 2026 application. Keep it displayed at the place of business;
   New York assigns the filing frequency in the online account.
2. **Collect.** In Shopify Admin → **Settings → Taxes and duties → United
   States → Collect sales tax → New York**, enter the sales tax ID (done
   November 24, 2026). Shopify calculates the rate for each New York address
   (about 7% to 8.875% in NYC).
3. **Check the clothing exemption.** Clothing under $110 an item is exempt from
   the 4% state rate and, in NYC, from local tax too; some counties still charge
   their local share. Doormats, mugs, flags, pins and stickers are taxed.
   Shopify applies the exemption from each product's category, so keep
   categories accurate (see [adding products](adding-products.md)). After
   enabling collection, quote a hoodie and a mug to a New York address.
4. **Stop paying tax on resold goods.** Printful holds a New York resale
   certificate (Form ST-120, submitted November 24, 2026) under **Billing → Tax
   information → Resale certificate**, so it stops charging the corporation
   sales tax on New York-bound orders once approved. Only orders after approval
   are exempt. Its legal name and address under **Billing → Legal info** must
   keep matching the certificate.
5. **File.** File every period New York assigns, including periods with no tax
   due. Shopify's tax reports supply the totals.

Orders shipped to New York before registration are an open accountant
question.

## Other states

Remote states require collection only above their economic-nexus thresholds,
typically $100,000 of sales or 200 orders a year into that state. Check
**Settings → Taxes and duties → United States**, where Shopify flags states
that are approaching a threshold, about once a quarter.

## International

Checkout collects no tax outside the US. Buyers in the International market pay
import tax and duties to the carrier on delivery, and the shipping policy says
so. Australia, New Zealand, Norway and Switzerland require registration only
above sales thresholds (Australia's is A$75,000 a year). The **United Kingdom**
expects overseas sellers to charge UK VAT on consignments of £135 or less from
the first sale; whether and how that applies here is an open accountant
question.
